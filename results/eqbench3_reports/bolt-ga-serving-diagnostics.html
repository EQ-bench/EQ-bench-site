All measured model conditions

What the diagnostics establish

  • All 10 GA files and 12 EA files match the NGC SHA-256 manifests. The language weights contain no nonfinite or zeroed tensors, have unchanged shapes, and show no obvious conversion-scale anomaly.
  • Actual API prompt token IDs match the checkpoint template in all eight checked prompts, including multi-turn and thinking-on/off cases.
  • An independent eager Transformers forward pass agrees with vLLM’s greedy next-token choices at 192/192 checked positions. This limited probe is distinct from the new full Transformers benchmark.
  • On the H100 official vLLM 0.26 CUDA 13 image, teacher-forced next-token choices also agree with the A6000 reference at 192/192 checked positions. Maximum absolute chosen-token log-probability difference is 0.107. Full benchmark results above provide the broader test.
  • The parser preserves final-answer text when replaying the same raw output in four checked cases. All 730 structured GA turns in the five-repeat studies retain their required sections; no major transcript loss was found.
  • All 355 audited thinking-on calls in the new repeat study contain reasoning. No capped or empty completions were found in the seven new GA repeats, and no extra thinking budget was supplied.
  • EA on the current CUDA 12.9 stack scores 57.35. On the 42 historical shared scenarios, EA scores 57.98 on CUDA 13 and 58.19 on CUDA 12.9. The driver change alone does not explain the EA-to-GA regression.

The old template is not an equivalent thinking-off condition

The EA template with GA weights was stopped before judging. Of 57 returned calls, 44 contained reasoning and 15 had empty final content despite requesting thinking off. These are response attempts, not scenario counts. This condition has no valid reported rubric score. It does not invalidate the clean GA-template baseline.

Where the behavioral regression appears

The largest EA-to-GA thinking-on loss is in the 19 analysis scenarios. GA’s five-run mean is 47.82 for that group, versus 54.96 for the single current-stack EA run. Roleplay means are 57.83 and 59.50 respectively. This localizes an observed difference; it does not identify its training cause.

In scenario 406, GA repeatedly praises therapeutic boundary-setting in a conversation where the participant misreads exaggerated venting and alienates a friend. The saved EA answer identifies that misattunement. On the same prompt, GA thinking on averages 36.50 across five runs, GA thinking off 41.33, and EA thinking on scores 62.50 once. The H100 official-image run repeats the same interpretive failure and scores 37.50 on that scenario.

Sampling checks

Exploratory eight-scenario checks did not show a clear rescue: the existing five baseline subset scores average 55.78, with a 54.25–58.45 range. Removing min_p scored 56.75; temperature 1.0 without min_p scored 56.65. Those subset scores are not comparable to NVIDIA’s full benchmark score. Two subsequently started sampling runs were cancelled after changing the diagnostic plan, and count against the six-run allowance.

Offline sensitivity checks using analysis-only, nonanalysis-only, equal group weights, extra safety/boundary metrics, or all recorded style traits do not reproduce NVIDIA’s four-model pattern. These are diagnostic calculations, not replacement benchmark scores.
